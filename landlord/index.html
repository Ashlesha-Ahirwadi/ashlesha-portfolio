My thinking

  • Answer the owner's first question up top. Before trusting an AI with their customers, an owner wants to know how long setup takes and how much of their time it needs. Your FAQ already answers this, so I moved it into the headline.
  • Keep the animations, lose the gaps. The step animations are the best part of this section, but each one sits alone on a mostly empty screen. Here they play one after another in a single card, and the timeline on the left shows the whole setup at once. Click any step to jump to it.
  • One continuous story. The AI gets its name in step 1 and keeps it in the ring for every step after. It reads as a new teammate joining, not 9 separate features.
  • Show what the owner does. The 4 steps that need the owner are tagged "you". Everything else is the AI.
  • Easy to test. You already run Clarity, so comparing scroll depth and time on this section before and after would show whether it works.
