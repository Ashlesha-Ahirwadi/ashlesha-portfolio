My thinking

  • This section is about 7 screens long, roughly half the homepage. I'd bet most people never see the last step.
  • A storage owner wants to know two things: how long does setup take, and how much of my time? Your FAQ already answers that, so I put it right at the top.
  • I like your little animations, so I kept them. I just cut the empty space between them.
  • The AI gets its name in step 1 and stays in the middle the whole time, filling up as it learns. It feels like someone joining the team, not a list of features.
  • I tagged the 4 steps where the owner has to do something. The AI does the rest.
  • You already run Clarity, so it'd be easy to check if this works: compare scroll depth and demo clicks before and after.
